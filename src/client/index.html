How it works

  1. A finite kit, enough to finish

    Each scene comes with a fixed, counted set of parts: enough to build something you're pleased with on your own. When a part runs out, it's out, so where you put it matters.

  2. Any colour, any change

    Colour is never rationed: every part comes in every colour. Recolour, turn or take parts off whenever you like; a part you remove goes back into your kit.

  3. Kept, then shown

    Every change is saved on the server as you make it. Close the page, come back later, and your build is waiting.

Built together, from different kits

The plan is for a few people to work on one construction at the same time. The scene's kit is shared out between them, so each person holds different parts and the build needs everyone's.

  • Ask the group. Missing a part? Post a request, and anyone who has it can send some or all of what you need.
  • Leave a plan. Put a see-through part where something should go, labelled with its code, so whoever holds it knows exactly where it belongs.
  • Still complete alone. On your own you get the whole kit, and never have to wait for anyone.
